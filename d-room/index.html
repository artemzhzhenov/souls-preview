SCENE 03 · THE STORY

Tessa left Boston years ago, and she left without warning.

Since then, she has built a life that's fully hers.

Now the past shows up on her doorstep, in person.

Someone new has walked into her days, and things feel easy again.

The two men pulling at her heart are bound to each other for life.

Whichever way she turns, someone she loves ends up hurt.
